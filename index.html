About

I'm a computer engineering student graduating in May 2028. My focus is the hardware side of computing: I've programmed microcontrollers in C and assembly, built logic-gate circuits, described digital logic in Verilog, and modeled, simulated, and tested circuits with diodes, resistors, transistors, capacitors, and inductors. I've also done professional work: C++ socket programming and networking tasks at my internship.

I've genuinely loved what I've done in every course so far, and I like to stay versatile. I don't want to be boxed into one specialty, so this site shows a range: embedded systems, digital design, analog circuits, software, and the math and physics underneath all of it.

I like work that ends with something I can point to: a board that runs, a signal on a scope, a simulation that matches the math.

ECE 230 — Intro to Embedded Systems

Winter 2026 · Microcontroller-controlled IR and LED system

Programmed a PIC18F56Q24 microcontroller in C and assembly to drive an IR remote and LEDs from ADC inputs, timers, and PWM signals. The term covered memory, storage, instruction set architecture, interrupts, and power management.

PIC18F56Q24 C Assembly PWM ADC Interrupts

ECE 233 — Intro to Digital Systems

Fall 2025 · From logic gates to a working datapath

Built digital logic up from individual gates: combinational and sequential circuits, flip-flops, finite state machines, and registers. Designed a game from logic gates, implemented parts in Verilog, and built out a processor control structure with a data path and control path.

Verilog Digital logic FSMs Simulation FPGA

ECE 250 — Electronic Device Modeling

Spring 2026 · Modeling, simulating, and building analog circuits

Designed circuits with MOSFETs, BJTs, diodes, and capacitors, using transistors as amplifiers, switches, and logic gates. Covered the device physics of diodes and transistors, then modeled and simulated the circuits in PSpice before building them in the lab.
